# Places

A Place is the setting, the light, and the treatment. It is not the person. A Person is who. A Place is where and how.

A Place with nobody attached is a Shot without a stand-in person. Your pictures of a location set the mood. They are not copied into the Shot as a background plate.

## The library

**Places** in the top bar lists Scenri's 42 and the ones you save. **Keepers** is the shortlist. A Place you leave unfinished comes back as a draft.

## Create one

Open **Places** and start a new one. You can begin in four ways:

- **Words.** Describe the place.
- **Guide me.** Answer a few questions. Scenri treats that as a starting direction, not a picture to copy.
- **Pictures.** Add one to four pictures of the place. The more they share, the better it reads.
- **A Shot you already made.** Scenri reads the place in it and leaves the Product and the people out. If that Shot was made in a Place you already have, you can use that Place instead of reading a new one.

Scenri then writes what later Shots are told about the place. Read that, change it if it is wrong, name the Place, and save.

Drawing a preview of the Place needs image generation, the same way a Person does. [Connect image generation](CONNECT.md).

## Use it

In Create, type `/` and choose the Place. [Create a Shot](CREATE.md).

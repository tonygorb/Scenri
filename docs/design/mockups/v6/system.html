Non negotiables

IconsPhosphor-style strokes only, 1.8 to 2.2 weight, 11 to 15px. No emoji anywhere.
CopySentence case. No long dashes. Numbers stay numerals. Honest states: shimmer plus elapsed, never a fake percent.
Serif accentOne italic phrase per screen, in the top heading only. Never in buttons, body or labels.
GoldCredits pill, keeper star, generation shimmer. Appears nowhere else, both themes.
Motion120ms ease on hover and focus, 250ms on overlay open. Nothing bounces.

# Presenters

A Presenter is a person the brand uses again. The face is decided once, then `@` attaches that person to a Shot.

A Presenter is not a Scene. The Scene is the place and the light. The Presenter is who is in the picture.

## Before you start

Drawing a Presenter needs image generation. Photos do not skip that. If nothing is connected, the conversation stops and tells you. [Connect image generation](CONNECT.md).

Saving a Presenter does not draw again. Views already drawn can be saved after the provider is disconnected.

## The library

**Presenters** in the top bar lists Scenri's 19 and the ones you save. **Keepers** is the shortlist. A Presenter you leave unfinished comes back as a draft on this page.

## Create one

1. Open **Presenters** and start a new one.
2. Choose **Add photos** or **Describe someone**.
   Photos: one clear face, and up to three more angles if you have them. Scenri reads the face from them. It does not save the upload itself as the Presenter.
   A description: age, hair, build, skin, presence. One or two sentences is enough. Scenri does not draw a named real person from a description of who they are.
3. Approve the face. If it is wrong, say what should change and try again.
4. Scenri can draw further views from that face, including back and profile when you ask.
5. Name them and save.

Every later edit is a new version. You can step back.

## Use them

In Create, type `@` and choose the Presenter. The chip sends the approved views as reference images, within what your provider can carry. [Create a Shot](CREATE.md).

# Quick start

One Shot, using what Scenri already includes. Add your own Product, Person, and Place after this works.

## Before you start

- [Install Scenri](INSTALL.md) and leave it open.
- [Connect image generation](CONNECT.md). Codex on a paid ChatGPT plan, or an OpenRouter key if your Shot names a Product or a Person. Until something is connected, Generate waits.

## Make one Shot

1. Point Scenri at a Brand. Paste a website, or choose **Start from scratch** and name it.
2. Open **Create**.
3. In the line, type `$` and choose a Product. The library Products are enough for this first one.
4. Optionally type `@` for a Person and `/` for a Place.
5. Write a short direction after the chips. Portrait, one Shot, Standard is already selected.
6. Press **Generate**.

The Shot appears as its own card. Open it to see the picture and the line that made it.

## If Generate does nothing

The composer notice is the setup. [Connect image generation](CONNECT.md) walks through Codex and keys. [Troubleshooting](TROUBLESHOOTING.md) covers a key the provider rejected and a Codex CLI that is too old.

# Trademarks

The name **Scenri**, the Scenri wordmark, and the Scenri symbol identify the
official project maintained in this repository. They are not covered by the
software license: AGPL-3.0 gives you the code, not the identity.

## What you can do without asking

- Use the name to refer to the project truthfully: "built on Scenri",
  "a fork of Scenri", "compatible with Scenri", "imports `.brand` files".
- Keep the name in unmodified copies you redistribute, including `npx scenri`
  mirrors of the published package.
- Use the name in articles, talks, tutorials, and package descriptions.

## What needs a different name

- A modified fork you distribute or host as a product. Call it something else,
  and say it is derived from Scenri if you like. Do not present it under the
  Scenri name or logo.
- A hosted service offering this software. "Powered by Scenri" is fine;
  naming the service Scenri, or anything confusingly close, is not.
- Anything implying the project endorses, certifies, or operates your work.

## The mark files

The wordmark and symbol live in `apps/studio/brand/` and `docs/media/`. They
ship so the app can render its own identity, not as raw material for yours. A
fork should replace them; each is referenced from a handful of obvious places.

## The format is different

`.brand` is an open format under Apache-2.0. Saying your tool "supports
`.brand`" or "implements the Scenri brand format" is exactly the kind of use
the format exists for.
